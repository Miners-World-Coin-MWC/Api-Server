🎯 Custom (vanity) address

Generates a normal MWC private key whose address starts with your prefix. MWC addresses always start with 9 — that's added automatically and doesn't count toward your limit. Up to 10 characters after the 9. Runs locally in this tab; each extra character multiplies the search by ~58×, so this can get slow fast.

Target: 9 — type a prefix to see the expected number of attempts.
